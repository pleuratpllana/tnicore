# Project Brief — Interactive Portal Website

## Project overview

Create a single-page, dark, monochromatic website centred on a rotating mechanical navigation wheel inspired by a science-fiction Stargate or portal.

The wheel is the primary navigation interface and the defining visual feature of the experience. The site should feel like a premium, purpose-built technology interface rather than a conventional business landing page or a generic sci-fi game screen.

## Client's original requirements

- One-page website.
- Links to:
  - Onboarding for customers, clients and technicians.
  - Payment Portal.
- Dark theme using greys, blacks and other dark tones.
- Navigation links presented on a wheel inspired by a Stargate.
- The wheel should rotate.
- Clicking a link should simulate a jump into hyperdrive.
- The initial version will be improved in a later phase.
- The design and website must be coded.
- The site should be easy to navigate.
- The designer may choose the font.
- Use greyscale colours only.

## Interpreted navigation structure

The brief identifies two primary destinations:

1. **Onboarding**
   - Customer
   - Client
   - Technician
2. **Payment Portal**

Treat Customer, Client and Technician as choices within the Onboarding destination, not as additional top-level destinations, unless the client clarifies otherwise.

Use configurable destination URLs. If the real URLs have not yet been supplied, use clearly identified temporary routes or placeholders. Do not invent real portal URLs or imply that payment or onboarding functionality exists when it does not.

## Primary experience

1. The visitor arrives at a dark, atmospheric interface.
2. The central wheel is revealed.
3. The wheel rotates gently while the interface remains easy to understand.
4. The visitor selects a destination.
5. The selected destination receives a clear visual response.
6. A coordinated hyperdrive transition plays.
7. The visitor is taken to the configured destination.

## Scope for the initial phase

### In scope

- Complete single-page visual experience.
- Designed and implemented rotating wheel.
- Clear, functional navigation.
- Onboarding destination choices for Customer, Client and Technician.
- Payment Portal destination.
- Hyperdrive-style selection and transition animation.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts.
- Accessible interaction states and reduced-motion handling.
- Temporary destination routes or placeholders where final URLs are unavailable.

### Out of scope unless separately requested

- Building the actual customer, client or technician onboarding systems.
- Building or integrating a payment gateway.
- Authentication, user accounts or backend services.
- Payment processing or collection of sensitive payment information.
- Additional pages or portal functionality beyond the navigation experience.

## Success criteria

- The wheel is visually distinctive and clearly functions as navigation.
- Visitors can understand the available destinations without guesswork.
- The wheel's motion feels controlled, smooth and intentional.
- Selecting a destination triggers a polished, coherent transition.
- Navigation remains usable if animations are reduced or unavailable.
- The experience works across common desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- The implementation is maintainable and does not rely on a static screenshot as the wheel.
