# Visual References and Client Assets

Place approved project references and supplied client assets in this folder.

Suggested organisation:

- `wheel/` — screenshots and references for wheel geometry and mechanical structure.
- `interface/` — typography, navigation and interface references.
- `hyperdrive/` — transition, speed and portal-effect references.
- `brand/` — client logo, brand assets and supplied fonts.

## Guidance

- Use references to communicate specific visual qualities, not as assets to copy wholesale.
- Prefer clear screenshots or images with a short, descriptive filename.
- Keep original client assets separate from inspiration images.
- Do not invent or modify the client's logo.
- Confirm usage rights before including third-party images, fonts or other assets in the delivered website.
- If no logo is supplied, use a restrained typographic placeholder until the client provides one.


Reference interpretation:

Study all supplied images carefully. They are visual references for different aspects of the experience, not assets to place directly into the website.

Pay particular attention to:
- The actual mechanical construction and proportions of the Stargate-inspired ring.
- Concentric structures and independently rotating components.
- Raised mechanical details, segmented surfaces and engraved markings.
- Metallic material quality, shadows and depth.
- The spatial movement and acceleration of the hyperdrive references.

Combine these characteristics into one cohesive, original interface.

IMPORTANT: The final website must remain strictly monochromatic. If any reference contains blue, red, amber or other colours, use it only to understand geometry, lighting placement or construction. Translate everything into black, charcoal, graphite, silver and white.
